# AI automation studio: research, naming and logo round 1

Checked 2026-10-11. Facts are marked **[verified]** (a tool returned it today) or **[assumption]**. This is preliminary screening, not legal clearance.

## 1. What the brief asked for, and what I added

From your brief: a credible AI automation studio, circuit / routing / machine-architecture logic, dark and restrained, no brains / robots / sparkles / infinity / cross shapes, and a mark that works as a flat shape before any glass or glow.
Your extra direction: logo first, Pinterest-style micro-graphics or vintage detailed logo work, and a name that feels legitimate.

How I reconciled "vintage detailed" with "must work at 16px": every concept has two layers.
- **Mark**: a bold flat symbol. Works at favicon size and in one colour.
- **Plate**: the detailed vintage layer (tick scales, rosettes, matrix grids, punch-hole rows, screws, fine rules). Used large: hero, stickers, stamps, packaging, business cards. The plate is never what makes the brand recognisable.

- **Hollerith Works**: the most "legit" and unusual, with a true story (punched cards were the first mass data automation). Fails on spelling and pronunciation, and the namesake IT consultancies.

## 6. Logo concepts (round 1, flat black first)

| | A Crossbar Works | B Signalbox Works | C Hollerith Works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | Signal bar crosses the open gate of a C and lands on a contact node | S built like curved track; slits are sleepers on one continuous route | Punched card with clipped corner; holes spell H |
| Construction | C with 38-unit arms, 20-unit bar, 23-unit node on a 256 grid | Two circles (r 44, band 30), eight radial slits, point-symmetric | 208 x 184 card, 36-unit chamfer, 13 holes on a 5 x 5 grid |
| Negative space | The gap between node and spine is the "unconnected" half | Slits | Holes are real knock-outs (evenodd) |
| Small size | **Weakest**: node and bar merge at 16px; the small cut helps only a little | Slits vanish and it becomes a clean S, which is graceful | Main mark dissolves at 16px; the **small cut (one big H cut-out) holds well** |
| Plate (vintage layer) | Nameplate with screws, crossbar matrix, route landing on the bar, ruler scale | Railway roundel: tick bezel, lamp-lens rays, type on the ring, one lit lamp | Punch-card ticket: micro-hole row, guilloche band, printed digit strip |
| Animation potential | Route lights up across the matrix, node closes | Slits shuttle along the track | Holes punch in one by one to write the name |

Honest critique:
- **A is the weakest mark.** C-plus-bar reads a little like a door handle or a clamp, and it is the least distinctive silhouette. It wins on name, not on drawing. It needs another round.
- **B is the most ownable and attractive mark** and degrades gracefully, but the slit idea is tied to the letter S.
- **C is the most vintage** and has the best story, but the card silhouette can read as a SIM or SD card, and the name is the hardest to say.

Files are in `logo-design/svg/` (symbols, small cuts, lockups, plates) and `logo-design/renders/`. Lockup and plate type is outlined from Windows system fonts (Bahnschrift, Rockwell, Engravers MT) **for exploration only**. Those are not licensed for final logo use; the kit phase would swap in open-licence equivalents.

## 8. Round 2: "One Click Works" decal (replaces the Crossbar emblem)

Direction change from you: show what AI automation does (one click, whole complex job runs, like n8n), retro and micrographic, and not like other AI brands. Name is a placeholder; availability **not checked**.

Research (firecrawl, 2026-10-11):
- AI-logo sameness is documented: circular shape, gradient, central opening, radiating elements, soft organic curves, sparkle icons (velvetshark.com/ai-company-logos-that-look-like-buttholes; FastCompany's "swirling hexagon" trend; lefthd.com/insights/why-ai-logos-look-alike). Suggested exits: sharp angles, no gradients, avoid radial symmetry.
- Micrographics (itsnicethat.com/features/forward-thinking-graphic-trends-2026-graphic-design-120126): technical information as texture, from industrial diagrams and decals: grids, tiny symbols, typographic overlays; extra details should carry meaning.
- Pinterest and Instagram pages could be found by search but not scraped (blocked), so I worked from the descriptions, not from images.

Design logic: asymmetric chamfered plate (not a circle), flat ink plus one signal colour (no gradients), push-button on the left starts a five-step chain (catch, read, decide, route, done) that mirrors a workflow trigger-to-result. Every micro-detail is functional: numbered tiles, patent-style reference numerals, spec table (1 click in, 1 result out), scale rule. Files: `svg/occ-primary-*.svg`, `svg/occ-stamp-*.svg` (paper and dark).
